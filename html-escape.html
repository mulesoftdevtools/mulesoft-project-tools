HTML Entity Encoder & Decoder

Escape characters that have special meaning in HTML so user-supplied text can be rendered safely, or decode a string full of entities back into readable text. Also useful when embedding XML or HTML fragments inside another document.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose Escape (text → entities) or Unescape (entities → text).
  2. Paste your content — conversion runs as you type.
  3. Escaping converts < > & " ' into their entity equivalents.
  4. Tick Escape all non-ASCII to convert accented and non-Latin characters into numeric entities too.
