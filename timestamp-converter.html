Epoch & Timestamp Converter

Convert Unix epoch timestamps (seconds or milliseconds) into readable dates in both your local time zone and UTC, or go the other way and turn a date into an epoch value. Includes a live clock and relative-time descriptions like “3 hours ago”.

How to use this tool

  1. To decode a timestamp: paste the epoch value into Timestamp → date. Seconds and milliseconds are detected automatically.
  2. To encode a date: type a date into Date → timestamp, or press Now to use the current moment.
  3. Both directions show the result in your local time zone, in UTC, and as an ISO 8601 string.
  4. Every value has its own copy button, so you can grab exactly the format you need.
