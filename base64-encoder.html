Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode text to Base64 and decode it back again. Unlike a naive btoa() call this handles the full Unicode range correctly, so accented characters, emoji and non-Latin scripts survive the round trip. URL-safe Base64 (used by JWTs) is supported too.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose Encode or Decode.
  2. Paste your text or your Base64 string — conversion runs as you type.
  3. Tick URL-safe to use the - and _ alphabet without padding, which is what JWTs and query parameters use.
  4. Use Swap to move the output back into the input and flip the direction — handy for verifying a round trip.
